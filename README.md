## 📜 Privacy Policy Structure

Each app’s privacy policy includes:

1. **Introduction** – Overview of the app and its privacy principles.
2. **Information Collection** – Details about what data is collected (if any).
3. **Permissions** – Camera, gallery, or storage access explanations.
4. **Data Usage** – How the app uses collected data.
5. **Data Storage & Security** – Local or cloud storage and security measures.
6. **Third-Party Services** – Services used and their privacy compliance.
7. **Ads & Monetization** – Information on advertising, if applicable.
8. **Children’s Privacy** – Compliance with laws for users under 13.
9. **Data Deletion & User Rights** – How users can manage or delete their data.
10. **Updates & Changes** – How policy updates are communicated.
11. **Contact Information** – Support email and company contact details.
